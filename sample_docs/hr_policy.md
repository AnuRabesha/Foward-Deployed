# HR Policy Manual

## Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Full-remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed annually.

## Leave Policy
All full-time employees receive 20 days of PTO annually. Unused PTO up to 5 days may be carried over to the following year.

## Code of Conduct
All employees must complete annual ethics training. Violations of the code of conduct are subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination.
