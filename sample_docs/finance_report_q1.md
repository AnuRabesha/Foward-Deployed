# Q1 Finance Report

## Revenue Overview
Total revenue for Q1 reached $4.2M, a 12% increase year-over-year. The growth was driven primarily by enterprise subscriptions and professional services.

## Cost Analysis
Operating costs remained stable at $2.8M. Infrastructure costs decreased by 8% due to cloud optimization initiatives implemented in December.

## Forecast
Q2 projections estimate $4.6M in revenue based on current pipeline and renewal rates. Key risks include delayed enterprise deals and increased competition in the SMB segment.
